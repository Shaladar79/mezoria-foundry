# Mezoria Foundry v2 — Siphoning Strike (Q499–Q526)

> **Approved design record, 2026-11-14. Documentation only.** Current Foundry system version remains **0.2.17**. Numerical design is limited to Rank 1 Quartz, Rank 2 Garnet and Rank 3 Amethyst. This is a tabletop design record, **not a claim of Foundry implementation**. Q527 (Basic Duration compatibility) has **not** been answered. Q440 (Guardian's Intervention Basic Action Score) remains **unapproved**.

## Identity and base ability (Q499–Q508)

- **Name:** Siphoning Strike.
- **Mark:** Striker; **Reaver Precursor** organizational tag. It belongs to the **shared Striker ability pool**, so **all Strikers** may purchase it regardless of specialization.
- **Attack:** Normal **melee weapon** attack only; not ranged or unarmed. The base ability adds **no Physical damage** beyond normal weapon damage.
- **Base activation:** **2 Mana**, **2 total Action Score**, **no cooldown**. Repeated uses are permitted within the ordinary action economy if costs can be paid.
- **Healing source:** Only **actual enemy LifeForce damage inflicted** by the initiating attack after the normal Resistance → Magical Shield → Armor → LifeForce damage sequence. Damage mitigated by Resistance or absorbed by a Magical Shield or Armor does not count.
- **Base siphoning conversion:** **25%** of actual LifeForce damage, rounded **down**, with **minimum 1 LifeForce healed if actual LifeForce damage is positive**; zero damage yields zero healing.
- **Healing formula:** for damage D > 0, generated healing = max(1, floor(0.25 × D)); for D = 0, healing = 0.
- **Overhealing:** Restoration never exceeds the user's normal maximum LifeForce. Unused healing is ordinarily discarded; the **Vitae Barrier** exception below converts eligible excess healing into shielding only when the enhancement is selected. Excess healing does not otherwise create temporary LifeForce, a shield, or Wound recovery.

## Rank 2 Specialized: Vitae Barrier (Q517–Q524)

- Q517 originally approved the concept **Vitality Overflow**; Q519 renamed it **Vitae Barrier** and modified the concept to **excess-healing-only** Magical Shield generation.
- **Unlock:** Rank 2 Garnet; **purchase:** **2 EP**; **activation surcharge:** **+2 Mana**.
- **Activation:** Individually **optional**, must be declared and paid **before the Siphoning Strike attack roll**; the surcharge is spent even on a miss or with no excess healing.
- **Effect:** Convert **only excess healing generated by this Siphoning Strike** (healing left after restoring missing LifeForce) into **Magical Shield points, 1:1**. Healing actually used to restore LifeForce does **not** also create shield points. With zero excess, no new shield points are generated.
- **Shield framework:** Uses the existing Magical Shield damage layer, the restriction of **one active Magical Shield**, and the existing shield replacement framework. Do not introduce a separate temporary-LifeForce layer.
- **Duration:** Until **shield depletion or combat end**, whichever occurs first.
- **Repeated Vitae Barrier applications:** Compare the **existing Vitae Barrier's remaining shield points** with **newly generated points**, retaining the **higher value**. Do **not** add shield values together. Other-shield interactions beyond existing general rules are **not** newly specified.
- **Example:** At 38/40 LifeForce, attack generates 5 healing: restore 2 LifeForce, and if Vitae Barrier was selected, generate a 3-point Magical Shield. A subsequent 2-point barrier does not reduce an existing 3-point Vitae Barrier.

## Rank 3 Specialized: Greater Siphon (Q511–Q515)

- **Name:** Greater Siphon, approved Q515; **unlock:** Rank 3 Amethyst.
- **Purchase:** **3 EP**; **activation surcharge:** **+2 Mana**.
- **Effect:** Increase Siphoning Strike's LifeForce healing conversion from **25% to 50%** of actual enemy LifeForce damage. Continue the existing round-down and minimum-1-for-positive-damage rules, normal maximum LifeForce limit, and Vitae Barrier excess-healing exception.
- **Activation:** Individually **optional**; declare and pay **before the attack roll**. Mana remains spent if attack misses or deals no LifeForce damage.
- **Combination:** Greater Siphon and Vitae Barrier **can both be activated on one Siphoning Strike**. When both are activated, compute 50% siphoning, apply actual LifeForce restoration up to the maximum, and convert only the remaining excess healing into Vitae Barrier shield points.
- **Future progression:** Additional enhancement progression opportunities every **third Rank** (e.g., Ranks 6 and 9), but **no Rank 4+ numerical effects are approved**.

## Approved Basics and activation (Q509, Q510, Q516, Q525, Q526)

Only the following Basic enhancement compatibility has been positively approved:

| Basic | Quartz (Rank 1) | Garnet (Rank 2) | Amethyst (Rank 3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damage | +1 Physical/attack success | +2 Physical/attack success | +3 Physical/attack success |
| Accuracy | +3 percentage points to initiating attack TN | +6 percentage points | +9 percentage points |
| Cumulative EP **per** Basic | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| Added activation Mana **per** Basic | +1 | +2 | +3 |

- Basic Damage's additional damage is subjected to normal mitigation; only the resulting **actual LifeForce damage** counts toward healing.
- Tier values are **replacements, not additive purchases of earlier tiers**.
- **Purchased Basic Damage and Basic Accuracy are always active at purchased tier**, and their Mana surcharges are **mandatory**. They cannot be disabled or downgraded per activation to save Mana.
- **Basic Non-Damaging Potency:** **incompatible** (Q516).
- **Basic Action Score:** **incompatible** (Q526); the 2 total Action Score activation cost is not a positive Action Score effect.
- **Basic Duration (Q527), Physical Range, Movement and other unreviewed Basics:** **not approved or rejected**; no extra compatibility is inferred. The user chose to record the ability and move on rather than finish the audit.

## Mana examples and EP budgets

**Without mandatory purchased Basic surcharges:**

| Selected Specialized enhancements | Activation Mana | Healing conversion |
|---|---:|---:|
| Neither | 2 | 25% |
| Vitae Barrier | 4 | 25%; excess may become Magical Shield |
| Greater Siphon | 4 | 50% |
| Both | 6 | 50%; excess may become Magical Shield |

Any purchased Basic Damage and Accuracy surcharges **add** to the table's Mana values. All activations cost **2 total Action Score** and have **no cooldown**.

Per-ability **cumulative EP budget** is **1 at Rank 1, 3 at Rank 2, 6 at Rank 3**. Vitae Barrier (2 EP) plus Greater Siphon (3 EP) costs **5 of 6 EP at Rank 3**, leaving 1 for an approved Basic tier or banking. Specialized slots are assigned to Rank 2 and Rank 3; purchase is not automatic.
