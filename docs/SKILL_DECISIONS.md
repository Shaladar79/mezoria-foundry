# Mezoria — Skill Decisions

## Finalized Might skill roster (approved)

- **Athletics:** Strength-based movement, jumping, hauling, swimming, and climbing.
- **Mining:** Excavating ore and rock with physical tools.
- **Woodcutting:** Felling trees and physically processing timber.
- **Brawn:** Feats of raw strength, lifting, pushing, and breaking obstacles.
- **Grappling:** Controlling, restraining, and wrestling opponents.
- **Unarmed Combat:** Attacks using fists, kicks, and body strikes.
- **Heavy Weapons:** Attacks with two-handed melee weapons.
- **Intimidation:** Coercion through imposing physical menace; social manipulation involving authority or rhetoric may instead use Presence.

## Finalized Prowess skill roster (approved)

- **Acrobatics:** Balance, tumbling, rolls, and controlled falls.
- **Stealth:** Moving quietly and hiding from detection.
- **Sleight of Hand:** Palming, concealing, and precisely manipulating objects; not pickpocketing.
- **Pickpocketing:** Stealing carried or worn items directly from people.
- **Lockpicking:** Opening mechanical locks without keys.
- **Escape Artist:** Escaping physical restraints, bindings, or tight confinement.
- **Melee Weapons:** Attacks with one-handed melee weapons, including light and finesse weapons.
- **Ranged Weapons:** Attacks with bows, crossbows, slings, and other non-thrown ranged weapons.
- **Throwing Weapons:** Attacks with thrown knives, axes, javelins, and similar weapons.
- **Riding:** Controlling mounts, difficult maneuvers, and mounted movement.

## Combat and boundary decisions

- Dual Wielding is a Style Talent, not a Skill Talent.
- One-handed weapons use Melee Weapons (Prowess); two-handed melee weapons use Heavy Weapons (Might).
- Grappling and Unarmed Combat remain distinct Might skills.
- Climbing is covered by Athletics (Might), not a separate Prowess skill.
- Pickpocketing and Sleight of Hand are separate Prowess skills.
- Ranged Weapons remains a broad Prowess skill; Throwing Weapons is separate.
- Prowess already contributes to Dodge and Action Score; skill choices do not inherently increase either derived statistic.
- **Intimidation boundary:** Might governs physically imposing threats; social influence without a strength-based threat should be resolved under a suitable Presence skill when those skills are finalized.
- **Riding boundary:** Riding governs mount control. Care, training, and calming animals remain Animal Handling, whose attribute is not yet assigned.
- Weapon categories define skill scope; attack rules, defense mechanics, and future Style Talents remain separate decisions.

## Pending

- **Endurance:** finalized with Survival, Breath Control, and Resilience.
- **Intellect and Presence:** pending review. Concentration is an Intellect candidate, not yet finalized.
- Confirm the remaining background-granted skills' governing attributes in later passes; do not silently reassign them.

## Skill Talent progression

Talent +5 percentage points, Expertise +5 additional, Mastery +5 additional.

## Finalized Endurance skill roster (approved)

- **Survival:** Finding food, water, shelter, and enduring wilderness conditions.
- **Breath Control:** Holding and regulating breath during underwater activity or limited-oxygen conditions.
- **Resilience:** Enduring environmental extremes, deprivation, and physical hardship.

### Exclusions and boundary decisions

- Stamina and Pain Tolerance were removed from the proposed Endurance roster.
- Concentration was reassigned as a candidate for Intellect review; it is not yet an approved skill.
- Resilience must not grant automatic additional Health, Wounds, or passive resistance; such mechanics require separate approval.

## Crafting skill structure (approved; individual roster under review)

- Crafting is divided into separate specialized skills; there is no universal Crafting skill.
- Each approved discipline has its own Talent, Expertise, and Mastery progression.
- Retain exact background-granted skill names where possible to avoid breaking compatibility with `data/backgrounds.json`.
- **Individual crafting roster proposed, not yet approved:** Blacksmithing, Carpentry, Masonry, Leatherworking, Tailoring, Jewelcrafting, Tinkering, Alchemy, Cooking, Brewing, Scribing, Engineering, Shipbuilding, Herbalism.
- Do not automatically assign every trade skill to Intellect; review its governing attribute during Intellect development.
- Distinguish Engineering from Tinkering, Herbalism from Alchemy, and Scribing from magical inscription work.
- Magic Theory is the proposed replacement name for Arcana; Ritual Magic and Use Magic Device are additional Intellect candidates pending roster approval.

## Crafting discipline roster finalized (approved)

The separate crafting-skill model and all **14** disciplines below are approved as individual skills with independent Talent / Expertise / Mastery progression. Governing attributes remain pending review; this decision does not finalize the Intellect roster.

- **Blacksmithing:** Forging metal weapons, armor, tools, and hardware.
- **Carpentry:** Working wood into furniture, structures, and equipment.
- **Masonry:** Cutting, shaping, and constructing with stone.
- **Leatherworking:** Producing leather armor, saddles, bags, and other leather goods.
- **Tailoring:** Sewing clothing and textile goods.
- **Jewelcrafting:** Cutting gems and creating jewelry and ornamentation.
- **Tinkering:** Creating and repairing small mechanisms and intricate devices.
- **Alchemy:** Formulating potions, reagents, and alchemical substances.
- **Cooking:** Preparing meals, preserved foods, and other dishes.
- **Brewing:** Making fermented drinks and other brewed beverages.
- **Scribing:** Producing manuscripts, records, and ordinary scrolls.
- **Engineering:** Designing and constructing complex machines and systems.
- **Shipbuilding:** Constructing and repairing boats and other watercraft.
- **Herbalism:** Identifying, harvesting, and preparing useful herbs.

- **Inscription** is reserved for later magical crafting design and is **not** currently an approved skill.
- Ordinary **Scribing** and magical **Inscription** remain distinct.
- Background-granted skill names are preserved exactly; no background records are modified by this approval.

## Crafting governing attributes finalized (approved)

Each crafting skill has one default attribute; the GM may substitute another attribute when a specific task calls for a different aptitude, without changing the craft's Skill Talent or granting extra proficiency.

- **Blacksmithing:** Might.
- **Carpentry:** Prowess.
- **Masonry:** Might.
- **Leatherworking:** Prowess.
- **Tailoring:** Prowess.
- **Jewelcrafting:** Prowess.
- **Tinkering:** Intellect.
- **Alchemy:** Intellect.
- **Cooking:** Intellect.
- **Brewing:** Intellect.
- **Scribing:** Prowess.
- **Engineering:** Intellect.
- **Shipbuilding:** Intellect.
- **Herbalism:** Intellect.

- **Distribution:** Might 2 crafts; Prowess 5 crafts; Intellect 7 crafts; Endurance and Presence zero crafts.
- The base Might, Prowess, and Endurance rosters remain intact. Crafting skills are additional skills under their assigned attributes, not replacements.
- General Intellect skills are still under review; Inscription remains reserved, not approved.

## Finalized non-crafting Intellect skills (approved)

The 16 non-crafting Intellect skills below are approved, in addition to the seven previously approved Intellect crafting skills:

- **Magic Theory:** Understanding magical energies, spell structures, enchantments, and magical phenomena.
- **Ritual Magic:** Preparing, conducting, and maintaining magical rituals.
- **Use Magic Device:** Activating and operating enchanted items, artifacts, and magical equipment.
- **Concentration:** Maintaining mental focus during distractions, disruptions, and spellcasting.
- **Investigation:** Examining clues, evidence, and unexplained circumstances.
- **History:** Knowledge of civilizations, historical events, and figures.
- **Medicine:** Diagnosing illness, treating injuries, and administering care.
- **Navigation:** Determining direction, plotting routes, and reading maps.
- **Nature:** Knowledge of plants, animals, ecosystems, and natural environments.
- **Research:** Locating, evaluating, and interpreting information.
- **Appraisal:** Assessing item quality, authenticity, and approximate value.
- **Religion:** Knowledge of faiths, deities, sacred traditions, and theology.
- **Law:** Knowledge of laws, legal institutions, and legal procedures.
- **Cartography:** Drawing maps and conducting geographic surveys.
- **Forgery:** Creating convincing counterfeit documents, seals, and credentials.
- **Tactics:** Understanding battlefield formations, positioning, and strategic planning.

**Intellect total: 23 approved skills** (16 non-crafting, 7 crafts).

### Deferred / boundaries
- **Animal Lore** is held for the later dedicated Lore-skill review; do not approve or merge it into Nature yet.
- **Inscription** remains reserved for later magical crafting design.
- Concentration is distinct from spell potency and does not automatically grant Mana, damage, or accuracy.
- Tactics does not automatically grant attacks, damage, or Action Score.
- Forgery defaults to Intellect for designing convincing documents; the GM may use Prowess for precision execution with the same skill proficiency.
- **Presence** remains the next attribute roster to review.

## Lore specialization decisions — 2026-11-05 (partial approval)

- Lore skills are **Intellect-based** and **trained-only**. No untrained Lore checks; each specialization requires at least its own **Talent** to attempt a Lore skill check.
- **Lore: Mythical Creatures** is replaced by **Lore: Dragons** (dragon-kind) and **Lore: Legends** (unique, one-off creatures). These two specializations are distinct.
- **Lore: Eternals** is the unified name for the two previously proposed Celestial and Demon creature categories. Celestials and Demons are not standalone Lore skills.
- All other suggested Lore categories remain under review; no full Lore roster has been finalized.
- Normal skill tier bonuses remain Talent +5, Expertise +10 total, Mastery +15 total.

## Lore magic terminology — 2026-11-05

- **Lore: Spirit Magic** replaces the proposed name **Lore: Divine Magic**.
- It covers magic associated with Spirits (deity-like entities), their manifestations, and related spiritual workings.
- This approves the naming change, not the complete magical Lore roster. Lore remains Intellect-based and trained-only.

## Creature Lore roster finalized — 2026-11-05

All eight Creature Lore specializations are approved as separate **Intellect** skills requiring **Talent or higher** to attempt Lore checks. No untrained Lore checks are permitted.

- **Lore: Beasts:** Knowledge of ordinary and extraordinary non-draconic beasts, wildlife, predators, and domesticated animals.
- **Lore: Dragons:** Knowledge of dragons, draconic varieties, behavior, abilities, and weaknesses.
- **Lore: Legends:** Knowledge of unique, one-of-a-kind legendary creatures and their individual histories and powers.
- **Lore: Monstrosities:** Knowledge of unnatural monsters, hybrids, and aberrant creatures.
- **Lore: Undead:** Knowledge of reanimated creatures, vampires, liches, and other undead.
- **Lore: Constructs:** Knowledge of golems, animated armor, and artificially created beings.
- **Lore: Plants:** Knowledge of sentient plants, carnivorous flora, and plant-based creatures.
- **Lore: Shapeshifters:** Knowledge of werecreatures, mimics, and transforming beings.

- **Lore: Beasts** replaces the proposed **Lore: Animals** name and resolves the earlier deferred **Animal Lore** candidate. Neither older name is a separate skill.
- **Lore: Dragons** and **Lore: Legends** replace the broad proposed **Lore: Mythical Creatures** category; Legends concerns one-off individual creatures.
- Creature Lore is finalized. Supernatural and magical Lore lists remain under review, including the previously named Eternals and Spirit Magic entries.

## Supernatural Lore roster finalized — 2026-11-05

All six Supernatural Lore specializations are **approved** as distinct Intellect skills. Each is **trained-only** (Talent minimum), with independent Talent / Expertise / Mastery progression.

- **Lore: Elementals:** Knowledge of elemental beings, elemental affinities, behavior, strengths, and weaknesses.
- **Lore: Spirits:** Knowledge of Spirits including deity-like entities, identities, histories, and spiritual hierarchies.
- **Lore: Eldritch:** Knowledge of eldritch entities, alien origins, and incomprehensible beings.
- **Lore: Astral:** Knowledge of astral entities, their realms, inhabitants, and properties.
- **Lore: Eternals:** Knowledge of Eternals, including both opposing manifestations of their shared supernatural origin.
- **Lore: Fae:** Knowledge of fae beings, courts, supernatural bargains, customs, and traits.

- **Lore: Eternals** replaces the proposed separate Celestial and Demon Lore; neither is a standalone skill.
- **Lore: Spirits** covers deity-like spiritual entities, distinct from Eternals and from the proposed magical specialization Lore: Spirit Magic.
- Creature Lore and Supernatural Lore are finalized. Magical Lore remains under review.

## Magical Lore roster finalized — 2026-11-05

All seven Magical Lore specializations below are approved as separate, trained-only Intellect skills, each with independent Talent / Expertise / Mastery progression:

- **Lore: Elemental Magic:** Knowledge of elemental magical energies, their interactions, and magical traditions.
- **Lore: Concept Magic:** Knowledge of magical phenomena involving Time, Space, Gravity, Order, Life, Death, Dream, Spirit, Fate, Chaos, Void, and Balance.
- **Lore: Eldritch Magic:** Knowledge of eldritch magical traditions, techniques, and manifestations.
- **Lore: Astral Magic:** Knowledge of astral magical energies, phenomena, and workings.
- **Lore: Spirit Magic:** Knowledge of magic associated with Spirits, deity-like beings, and related spiritual manifestations.
- **Lore: Soul Magic:** Knowledge of soul-based magical phenomena and Soul Weapon workings.
- **Lore: Ancient Magic:** Knowledge of lost magical traditions and forgotten magical techniques.

- Lore: Spirit Magic is the approved name replacing proposed Lore: Divine Magic; it is distinct from Lore: Spirits.
- Lore: Concept Magic is one skill for the overall Concept family rather than separate skills for each concept.
- Magical Lore describes specialized magical knowledge, distinct from practical Magic Theory, Ritual Magic, or Use Magic Device.
- All 21 Lore specializations are trained-only (Talent minimum; untrained Lore checks prohibited).
- Lore categories completed: Creature Lore 8, Supernatural Lore 6, Magical Lore 7.
- **Total approved skills: 91** (70 original core skills + 21 Lore skills); **Intellect: 47**.
- Magical Inscription remains reserved for later development and is not part of the approved roster.

## Crafting training gate — 2026-11-05 (approved)

- All **14 approved crafting skills require training**, with a minimum of **Talent** to attempt a check using that crafting skill.
- Characters with no training in a particular craft cannot attempt checks under that specialized skill. Training in one craft does not grant training in another.
- This rule applies to Blacksmithing, Carpentry, Masonry, Leatherworking, Tailoring, Jewelcrafting, Tinkering, Alchemy, Cooking, Brewing, Scribing, Engineering, Shipbuilding, and Herbalism.
- This is a training restriction for **skill checks**; routine everyday actions not requiring checks are not automatically prohibited.
- Other non-Lore, non-crafting training restrictions are still under review.
