# Mezoria — Background Decisions

> Background decision history, including proposals and later corrections. Current selectable definitions and bonuses are stored in [`../data/backgrounds.json`](../data/backgrounds.json); later decisions supersede earlier ones.

## Character backgrounds (approved framework)
- Defer remaining Action Score costs until after further character-creation work. The approved movement cost of 2 Action Score remains unchanged; no basic attack cost has been approved.
- Each character chooses **one Background** during character creation, from the categories **Common, Tradesman, Criminal, Social**.
- A Background grants an attribute allocation choice: **+4 percentage points to one attribute**, **or +2 percentage points to each of two distinct attributes**.
- Each Background grants **one Skill Talent** (the background selection already included in the initial Skill Talent allocation) and **one non-combat background ability**.
- Specific backgrounds within categories, their granted skill talents, non-combat abilities, and any other requirements remain to be designed individually.

## Military-background roster additions (approved)
- Add **Soldier**, **Town Guard**, and **Mercenary** to the **Common** Background category.
- Reserve **Military Engineer** as a **Tradesman** Background candidate, not as one of these Common additions.
- The earlier 20 Common Background names remain a **proposed roster under review**. These three additions bring the working candidate list to 23, but do not approve all 20 previous candidates automatically.
- Individual skill talents, attribute-benefit selection, and non-combat abilities for these backgrounds are not yet defined.

## Final Common Background roster (approved; supersedes proposed roster)
- The **20 approved Common backgrounds** are: Farmer, Rancher, Fisher, Hunter, Forager, Homesteader, Laborer, Villager, City Dweller, Servant, Stablehand, Courier, Sailor, Traveler, Wanderer, Refugee, Orphan, Hermit, Soldier, Town Guard.
- **Miner** and **Lumberjack** are removed from Common and await a decision on which other category to assign them to; do not automatically move either into Tradesman.
- **Mercenary** is removed from the proposed background roster.
- **Military Engineer** remains only a potential Tradesman background, not an approved selectable one.
- No additions requested. Next: flesh out each approved Common background individually with an assigned Skill Talent and a non-combat ability, while retaining the established character-selectable +4% to one attribute or +2% to two distinct attributes.

## Farmer Common Background (partial approval)
- **Approved:** Agriculture becomes a skill in the roster, with its governing attribute still to be defined. Farmer grants the **Agriculture Skill Talent (+5 percentage points)**.
- **Approved:** **Bountiful Harvest** (non-combat): identify soil quality, assess crop health, and recognize harvest readiness; gain **+10 percentage points** on checks involving cultivation, crop care, and suitability of farmland.
- **Attribute choices proposed, not yet approved:** +4 percentage points to **Endurance**, or +2 to **Might** and +2 to **Endurance**. Confirm whether each background restricts its attribute choices to thematic options.

## Farmer attribute bonus (approved)
- Farmer grants **+2 percentage points Might and +2 percentage points Endurance**.
- With the previously approved Agriculture Skill Talent (+5) and Bountiful Harvest non-combat ability (+10 on qualifying agriculture checks), the Farmer Common Background is fully defined.
- The previously proposed +4 Endurance alternative for Farmer is **not selected**.

## Rancher Common Background (approved)
