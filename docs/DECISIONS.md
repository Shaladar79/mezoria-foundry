# Mezoria Foundry v2 — Decision Index

Design decisions are now grouped by subject to make rules development and a future tabletop rulebook easier to navigate.

- [Rules Decisions](RULES_DECISIONS.md) — game foundation, skills, combat, defenses, damage, movement, and unresolved rules.
- [Ability and Elemental Decisions](ABILITY_ELEMENTAL_DECISIONS.md) — approved ability creation, enhancement progression, advanced elements, armor resistances, and penetration through Q109 (Q110 pending).
- [Race Decisions](RACE_DECISIONS.md) — racial roster and a readable guide to racial features.
- [Background Decisions](BACKGROUND_DECISIONS.md) — the background framework, selection history, and approved Common backgrounds.

## Canonical game data

- [`../data/core-rules.json`](../data/core-rules.json) — general system mechanics and character-creation references.
- [`../data/races.json`](../data/races.json) — full race, subgroup, and racial ability data.
- [`../data/backgrounds.json`](../data/backgrounds.json) — the approved Common background roster and definitions, with space for later categories.

## Migration note

The former combined `DECISIONS.md` has been split into the subject-specific files above. This page remains as a stable landing page for old links. Historical decisions, including superseded proposals, are retained in the topic-specific histories; **later revisions supersede earlier versions**.
