3 · Mark of Power

Choose two distinct Skill Talents from this Mark's list. A skill already granted by your background cannot be selected through your Mark.

4 · Three Free Skill Talents

Choose any three different approved skills without repeating racial, background, or Mark awards.

5 · Allocate Attributes

Begin at 35% each; distribute exactly 35 points, no more than +15 per attribute before bonuses. Racial and background bonuses are included in final percentages.

6 · Character Name, Validate and Save

Choose your character name here, as the final creator step.
